# interior-inspired

**What it is:** a single-page HTML concept (Dec 2025, made with Claude) whose palette is described in its CSS as "derived from the church interior": ceiling blue with a gold star diaper, frieze green, wall burgundy, oak, gold. Blackletter parish name in gold on the blue field; Guéranger reading; Mass, Office, and Confession times in three cards; upcoming feasts.

## Verdict

**Josh:** not singled out. Only `liturgical-system` was named as in the ballpark (2026-10-27).

**Agent assessment**

**Keep**
- **The principle of deriving color from the real building.** If OLA's interior really does have a blue, star-powdered ceiling, a green frieze, and burgundy walls ⚑ verify, that's a far better source than arbitrary choices. It's also very much in the spirit of Pugin's painted interiors.

**Avoid**
- Blackletter used as a costume on the parish name.
- Dark, heavy fields across the whole page, which push toward the muddy palette of the AI concepts.
- Times shown in generic cards, a standard church-site pattern.
- Invented schedule data that looks real. **Nothing in this file is a verified parish fact.**
