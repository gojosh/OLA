## What happened
- Josh chose concept A: "Take Folio (A) further." Recorded in the exploration's NOTES.
- Folio now has a frontal for the day, drawn after Pugin's frontals (plate 07) and palls (plate 74). It has:
  - side orphreys
  - an inscription band in textura, with public-domain texts (Coverdale, King James Version, the traditional English Requiem introit), cited in its caption
  - a fringe
  - roundels carrying the day's emblem, drawn after plate 63: saw and ship for Simon and Jude, crown for All Saints, cross for Sundays and All Souls, crown of thorns for Lent

- Sample days now cover the full week of 27 October to 2 November (including All Souls in black) plus Advent Sunday and a Lenten weekday.
- On phones the order is title, times, week, frontal, collect.
