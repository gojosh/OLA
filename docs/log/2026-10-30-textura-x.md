# 2026-10-30 — Pugin Textura: the modern x, now the x; Josh's archaic x and z

**Agent/tool:** Claude Code (Opus 5.5)

## What happened
- **The direction** (brief `2026-10-30-textura-x-z.md`). Josh: "can you export the archaic x, and also create a modern x? Something along these lines, but of course matching all of our style, widths, etc.", with a textura x as the example. Then: "Also export the z, which I will hand-draw".
- **The archaic x exported** (`references/export-x.svg`), as the f, a and t were: the built outline over Pugin's x, in plate pixels, with the family's lines.
- **The z exported** (`references/export-z.svg`). Pugin drew the z squeezed on the plate's second line (to 0.777 of the height), and the font's z stands at the full x-height, so the export script now handles that:
  - the canvas is the letter's own frame, at full height;
  - the layer `pugin` is his z stretched up to it, which the built z lies over;
  - the layer `pugin-as-drawn` is his z as it stands, beside it on the same baseline.
  - The SVG's comment gives the mapping back to the plate. The x's export came out the same from the changed script.
- **The modern x** (`textura_letters.x_modern`, first the glyph `x.alt`, now `x`).
  - **Its skeleton** is n's: two strokes crossing between where n's two stems stand. The thick one is n's first head, then straight down to the right to n's second foot, which turns out into its spur. The thin one is a head at the upper right, then straight down to the left to a foot at the lower left. The head, foot, angles and pen are the family's.
  - **The thin stroke.** Drawn between the stems, it ran 19° off the nib's edge at the top (14° at the foot, where the nib has turned), and the pen made it about 21 px, as heavy as the z's diagonal; the example has a hairline.
    - Tried: a lighter pressure (0.5 and 0.3 of the nib), the nib's tip (as the e's crossing member), and its ends spread out past the stems.
    - Chosen: the ends stand 25 px out past the stems (`thin.out`). The stroke then runs almost along the nib's own edge, and the pen itself makes it a hairline, 10 px near the head and 8 px near the foot, with no change of pressure. Its head and foot stand at the letter's corners, level with the thick stroke's, as in the example.
    - Also tried and dropped: an S-bend in the thick stroke, steeper by the head and foot; it wobbled.
  - **No spur on the thin foot**, as the example has none; one there would run back up along the hairline.
  - **An alternate, not the default.** It is reached through `ss04` ("Modern x"); every x is still Pugin's unless the set is on. Which should be the default is Q42.
  - **Built from n's measurements**, as u is. It was 292 units wide against n's 297 (now 291), and passes the outline checks.
  - **With `ss01`** its spur goes too. That needed an `ss01` rule placed after the alternates' sets (`sub x.alt by x.alt.nospur`), since the substitutions run in the order they are written.
- **Alternates built as letters are.** Until now an alternate had to be one of Josh's drawings. `BUILT_ALTERNATES` in `textura_letters.py` names the ones built by a construction; the build makes them with the letters Pugin didn't draw (`DERIVED`), and gives them their stylistic set as it does the drawn ones. The proof shows the modern x on its own, as a proposal, not over Pugin's x.
- **Each alternate spaced on its own.** Spaced together, adding the modern x moved Josh's y by a unit and nine of its pairs by 5. Now each alternate is fitted against the letters and itself, so nothing else moved: every other letter, side bearing and kerning pair is as before. The modern x has 57 kerning pairs of its own.
- **The proof** lists each letter once (y and x were listed twice), and sets some words with an x as they are and under `ss04`. `proof/x-modern.png` (not in git) shows it beside n, v, o and Pugin's x, and in words.
- **The modern x is the default** (brief `2026-10-30-textura-x-default.md`; decision 0015). Asked "Should the modern x be the default x?", Josh: "Yes, modern default".
  - **Renamed.** Pugin's x is now `x.archaic` throughout: its box in `source.json` (with `"char": "x"`), its reference images (`references/x.archaic.png`, `.body.png`, moved with git), its recipe (`recipes/x.archaic.json`, its at-bounds names renamed) and what it took alone in `family.json`. The modern x is `x` in `LETTERS` and `DERIVED`; `BUILT_ALTERNATES` now names `x.archaic` under `ss04` ("Archaic x"). `DERIVED_FORM` says what Pugin didn't draw ("modern x"), for the recipe, the build's report and the proof.
  - **Features.** `ss04` sets `x.archaic` from `x` and from `x.nospur`, so it holds with `ss01` on. `ss01` drops the modern x's spur (`x.nospur`).
  - **Spacing.** The modern x is spaced with the letters in their joint fit, and the archaic x after them, on its own. The other letters' side bearings moved by up to 2 units (21 of 27), and 90 kerning pairs not involving an x changed, most by 5 units.
- **Josh's archaic x** (brief `2026-10-30-textura-x-default.md`; decision 0014, amended). Josh: "My hand-drawn x is ready for import", drawn over `export-x.svg`.
  - **What he changed.** He kept the head and the upper stem. The flag curls down into a ball, the stroke down to the right from the middle ends in a spur, and the tail curls round into a ball, as Pugin's do. One shape.
  - **Placed** at one unit to the pixel, at (2603.876, 3321.564): 19 of the drawing's 42 nodes fall on the built outline's at that offset, to half a thousandth of a pixel.
  - **Built as it stands** (`x()` uses `OUTLINES["x.archaic"]`). It is 1.78 px from Pugin's x, against the construction's 4.66, and overlaps his ink 85% (72%).
  - In words (`proof/x-archaic-hand.png`, not in git) it sets as Pugin's form under `ss04`, also with `ss01`; the plain words have the modern x.
- **The modern x's thin stroke at 20 px** (brief `2026-10-30-textura-x-spread.md`). Shown the first trials again, Josh: "I like this one better", of the one with the thin stroke's ends 20 px out past the stems rather than 25.
  - `thin.out` starts at 20 (`x_modern`). The hairline is now 10–12 px, against 8–10; its head and foot stand a little in from the corners. The x is 282 units wide (291).
  - Only the x's outline changed. f's right and u's left side bearings moved by a unit through the joint fit; 64 kerning pairs changed, 50 of them with an x and the other 14 (f's and those before u) by 5 units.
- **A mistake in the modern x, fixed.** Josh: "Nope - you have the first leg of the top left stroke longer than what I showed."
  - **The cause.** Moving the trial into `x_modern`, the head's corner was put a head's length (25.6 px) right of where the trial had it: at n's first stem plus the head's run, not at the stem. So the head ran on at its own slant twice as far before the stroke turned down, and the stroke below it was steeper. Every modern x built today had it: the alternate, the default, and the 20 px version.
  - **Fixed** (`K1 = A + h * down`). The built x now lies on trial L to 0.0 px.
  - **Spacing.** The x is 283 units wide (282). e, t, b and y.alt moved by a unit; 48 kerning pairs changed, 28 of them with an x, the rest by 5 units.
- **The modern x more compact** (brief `2026-10-30-textura-x-compact.md`). Josh, of trial K: "Much better - seeing it in context, I think we need to the slightly more compact version."
  - `thin.out` starts at 15. The built x lies on trial K to 0.0 px. The thin stroke is 12–14 px (10–12 at 20 px). The x is 275 units wide (283).
  - i, p, y and j moved by a unit through the joint fit; 83 kerning pairs changed, 50 of them with an x and 33 others by 5 units.
- **Josh's z** (briefs `2026-10-30-textura-x-compact.md`, `2026-10-30-textura-z.md`; decision 0014, amended). He said it was "ready to go", but no `hand-drawn-z.svg` was in `references/` nor any new SVG in the project; asked to save it there, "It's there now".
  - **What he drew.** A new z: the upper stroke rising to a peak at the left and sweeping over to the right, the diagonal, and the lower bowl turning back along the baseline into a tail sweeping down to the left. Nothing of the built z is kept: none of its 20 nodes stands on the built z's.
  - **Placed** at one unit to the pixel, at (1418.718, 3302.445) in the letter's frame, where it overlaps Pugin's z stretched to full height (the export's layer) best: 91% (the built z, 34%); free to scale, it stays at 0.997.
  - **Its height.** Its peak stands 44 units above the heads (522 against 478) and its tail's point 18 below the baseline. On the plate Pugin's z peaks about 6 px above his y's heads; the export's stretch, from the built z's fit (squeeze 0.777, where the y's fit has 0.633), lifted it further, and Josh traced it so. In words (`proof/z-hand.png`, not in git) the peak stands a little above the heads.
  - **Built as it stands** (`z()` uses `OUTLINES`, keeping `second_line` to compare with Pugin's): 1.12 px from his z (the construction, 8.73).
  - **Spacing.** 278 units wide (218). Pairs before it close ("cz" 100 → 30, "ez" 85 → 20, "rz" 90 → 35); "zf" opens 0 → 55. The other letters moved by up to 2 units; 175 kerning pairs changed, 56 with a z, the rest by 5 units.
- **The z scaled down** (brief `2026-10-30-textura-z-scale.md`). Josh: "We actually need to scale it down - fix the bottom in place, and scale it down to the point it should be lowered to - we're slightly oversized."
  - **How far.** Pugin's z peaks 6.5 px above his y's heads on the plate (3547.2 against 3553.7, measured on his ink), on a line about 72% the height of the first: 9.0 px at ours, about 21 units. His y's foot runs on into its tail, so his second line's baseline can't be read off it; the heads can.
  - **How.** Scaled by 0.957 about its lowest point, the tail's point, held at (1426.527, 3538.241) in the letter's frame (`hand-drawn-z.json`: scale 0.9572, offset (1419.052, 3312.536)). Its peak now stands at 499 units (522); the lower bowl moved up 0.2 px.
  - **Against Pugin's**, through `second_line`: 3.66 px (as traced, 1.12). The fit that maps it onto his makes his taller than he drew it.
  - **Spacing.** 263 units wide (278). Five letters moved by a unit; 63 kerning pairs changed, 29 with a z ("zf" 55 → 30), the rest by 5 units.
- **Our alphabet under Pugin's** on the proof page (same brief). Below the plate's two lines, an image of ours at the same scale, each letter where his stands: those of his first line in his plate's own pixels, so each sits under his; y and z, which he squeezed onto the second line, at full height with their heads level with his there (the rule Josh first chose for his y: "top aligned"). Under his x is `x.archaic`; the modern x, u, w, j and y.alt aren't on the plate, and the ampersand isn't built. `proof/compare.png` (not in git) is the pair.
