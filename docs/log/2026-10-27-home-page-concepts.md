# 2026-10-27 — Two home page concepts

**Agent/tool:** Claude Code (Opus 5.5)

## What happened
- Josh asked for "a couple of draft home page concepts… pugin vector art, clean typsetting, generous whitespace… absolutely cutting edge design steeped in tradition."
- Studied the Glossary plates for motifs: cresting (19), diapering (23–27), alphabets (09), frontals (07), linen crosses (13–14), borders (15–16). Sampled the plates' colors with a script and white-balanced them against the paper, so the palette comes from Pugin rather than invented hex values.
- Built two self-contained concepts of the *Today* page, each with five sample days:
  - **A · Folio**: the home page as a page of a liturgical book. It has a plate frame, the day of the month as the folio number, rubrication, red-letter days, and cresting and an orphrey band that change with the day's rank. Set in Caslon.
  - **B · Tracery**: contemporary and architectural. The Christian year is drawn from the calendar as a rose window, one light per week, with a hand marking today. Textura is kept for days with names of their own. The day's services are shown as a timeline.
- Both apply vision principle 7: ornament is set by the day's rank, and Lent is shown as Lenten array (a proposal).
- Checked in the browser at desktop and phone widths: no sideways scrolling, and today's times move up on phones. Contrast was checked (see NOTES).
