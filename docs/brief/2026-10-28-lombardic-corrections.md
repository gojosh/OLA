# 2026-10-28 — Lombardic: lobed terminals and first corrections

Josh's words, verbatim, while the Lombardic E, F, L and T were being built.

On the lobed terminals:

> We should probably improve the two lobe and three lobe stems - some of them look quite sloppy in Pugin's original, FYI.

On the first five letters:

> A few corrections:
> On the top of the L: it almost looks like the curves aren't tangent where the stems meet the top of the L.
> The the bottom of the L: the bottom point on the right is supposed to be a rounded point, not a sharp point.
> On the F: the arm is supposed to have a sharp point on the top right side of the negative space in the lower portion, like it does on the top left side.
> On the T: The space between the sides of the T which drop down to stems, and the two stems on the base foot of the T are too close together - the original had more space, making it more legible at small sizes.
