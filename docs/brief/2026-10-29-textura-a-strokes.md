# 2026-10-29 — Textura: the a in three strokes

Josh's words, verbatim, after the a's upper bowl had been run into the stem as one rounded path.

> Closer. But notice you've rounded most everything and joined previously separate lines. This a would have been drawn with three strokes. The stem (which is slightly concave in the top segment), the bottom bowl, and finally the top bowl. Where each of these three strokes intersect is precise, to create a seamless look, and provides the sharper aspects of the otherwise flowing penmanship.
