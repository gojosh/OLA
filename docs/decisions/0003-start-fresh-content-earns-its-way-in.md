# 0003 — Start fresh: existing content earns its way in

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-27
- **Decided by:** Josh Goforth, with carte blanche from the pastor

## Context
The current site has 95 pages (83 published and reachable), most of them stale, duplicated, thin, or orphaned (see `docs/research/current-site-audit.md`). Josh: "we do not need to have everything or even most of what is currently on the site on the new site. Father has given me cart blanche." ([brief](../brief/2026-10-27-ia-kickoff.md))

## Decision
- The new site is designed from audience needs, not migrated from the old one.
- Existing pages are source material. A page, or a piece of one, carries over only if it serves a need in the new IA and someone will own and maintain it.
- Josh has decision authority over structure and content, delegated by the pastor.

## Consequences
- No agent should plan a page-for-page migration or a URL-for-URL mapping as the starting point. (Redirects for important old URLs are still needed at launch for search and bookmarks; that's a launch task, not an IA constraint.)
- The IA can be much smaller than the current site.
- Ministries, programs, and pages without an active owner may not appear on the new site at all.
