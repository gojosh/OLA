# 0006 — Sacraments: say what only we can say

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-27
- **Decided by:** Josh Goforth

## Context
General explanations of the sacraments are now one question away, from AI assistants and elsewhere. Josh asked what matters for *us* to say. `docs/sections/sacraments.md` §2 proposed five things only OLA can say. Josh: "5 part works, we can skip #4 for now." ([brief](../brief/2026-10-27-sacraments-and-terms.md))

## Decision
Each sacrament's page says these things, and little else:
1. **Here:** the facts of receiving it at OLA, structured, dated, and owned.
2. **How we celebrate it:** the rite in this tradition (its words, music, and place).
3. **A person:** who will walk with you, and how to reach them.
4. ~~**Witness.**~~ Deferred.
5. **Why, briefly,** in our voice, citing the *Catechism* for depth.

No long catechetical essays, video playlists, link lists, or apologetics.

## Consequences
- Most of the old site's catechetical sacrament pages have no successor.
- The *Here* facts need a content model (structured fields, an owner, a "last confirmed" date) before the pages can be written.
- "How we celebrate it" depends on which rites OLA uses and on licensing for quoting them (Q27).
- The heart of each page is Father's emphasis (Q26).
- Witness can return later by a new decision; collecting stories needs consent and a process.
- The page shape and the grouping in `sacraments.md` §3–4 remain proposals.
