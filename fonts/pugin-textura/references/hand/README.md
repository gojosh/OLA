# Josh's hand: references drawn over Pugin's

Where Pugin's ink can't show a shape on its own, Josh paints it. The first is the s's main stroke, whose two ends are merged in his ink with the fine stems that grow out of them (Josh: "Do you need me to use a brush tool and show you the correct terminal shapes?", 2026-10-29).

- `s-template.png`: Pugin's s in light grey, at 6 times the plate: 870 × 1410 px, its top left at plate (2330, 3310). A pixel (u, v) is plate (2330 + u/6, 3310 + v/6).
- `s-main.png`: the s's main stroke, painted solid black over the template, the same size, without the fine stems and pips. The fit uses it as the s's main stroke (`textura.Ref`, `keep="main"`).
